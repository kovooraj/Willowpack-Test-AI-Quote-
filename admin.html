    How it works
    What visitors see, what gets tracked, and what to check when something looks off.
    
        
    
          Customer flow
          Map
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    1
    Inline box
    Sits in your page. Visitor types what they’re packaging or taps a format.
    
        
    2
    Full-screen chat
    Opens on top of the same page (no new tab). The first message is already sent.
    
        
    3
    Close & come back
    Closing keeps the chat. A “Continue my quote” button brings it back.
    
        
    4
    Email links
    Quote emails link to your landing page with ?resume=, which reopens that chat automatically.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    How does a chat go from “hello” to an order?
          
      
            
    1. Who are you? Before any price, the assistant collects full name, company and business email. No details, no quote: the chat is only logged.
      2. 
            
    2. Salesforce check, in parallel. Within seconds the system looks the customer up (new vs existing is decided by Salesforce, not by what they say). New customers get a Willowpack Lead, tiered A/B/S/C with an owner in about 20 to 30 seconds.
      3. 
            
    3. The quote. Spec questions, then the live price from the Pricing Engine (the only source of prices).
      4. 
            
    4. Approved? Yes → Account + Contact (if new) and an Opportunity, then a Willowpack Portal quote linked to that Opportunity is emailed with a Portal login. No → the one-time 5% discount (if margin allows) → still no → a call with their rep, which hands the chat to the rep.
      5. 
            
    5. 2 days later. If the Portal quote isn’t ordered yet, the rep gets a Salesforce call task.
      6. 
          
    
          
    Open the Customer flow to click through every step, or the System map for how the systems connect.
    
          
    Rolling out: the Portal quote is live for @sinalite.com / @willowpack.com test emails only (stage Portal); real customers keep the Salesforce Quote until go-live. The discount-then-call flow, the Bounced email and the 2-day check are being built.
    
        
    What do the chat outcomes mean?
          
      
            
    • Active: the visitor replied in the last 2 hours.
      • 
            
    • Accepted: they approved a price. Account, Contact and Opportunity are in Salesforce and the Portal quote is sent.
      • 
            
    • Call booked: they booked a call with their rep; the rep takes it from there.
      • 
            
    • Abandoned: no reply for 2+ hours after giving name, company and email, before getting a price. Their Lead exists and they get a “Continue my quote” email an hour later.
      • 
            
    • Bounced + Lead (rolling out): they got a price and left without approving. Their Lead exists and they get a “negotiate or connect with Sales” email an hour later.
      • 
            
    • Anonymous (rolling out; shown as Bounced today): no reply for 2+ hours and no contact details. Logged only; nothing goes to Salesforce.
      • 
          
    
          
    Conversations shows the Salesforce Account (with its AI tier), the Lead and Opportunity as links once they exist, refreshed every 2 minutes.
    
        
    What happens when a customer approves a quote?
          
      
            
    • Salesforce: a new customer’s Lead becomes an Account + Contact (the Lead is retired); everyone gets an Opportunity owned by their tier’s rep.
      • 
            
    • Willowpack Portal: the customer’s company is found by Salesforce Account, exact email or email domain (never by name), or created (CA or US region from where they are, Credit Card terms, their rep). Anything ambiguous is created new and flagged to the rep.
      • 
            
    • The Portal quote carries the chat price (or the discounted price), the specs and the link to the Salesforce Opportunity, and is emailed with a set-password invite for new customers.
      • 
            
    • The rep gets the hand-off email with an Open Portal quote button, or a red [ACTION NEEDED] alert if something needs a human.
      • 
          
    
        
    What gets sent to Salesforce?
          
    Every chat records the landing page URL the visitor was on (not our chat URL) plus UTM Source, Medium and Campaign. UTMs come from the landing page’s own URL (e.g. from your ads). If the URL has no utm_campaign, the box’s Campaign tag is used. These are saved on the first message of a chat and pushed to Salesforce with the lead.
    
        
    Why can’t a customer see quotes they started on willowpack.com?
          
    Quote history is saved in the visitor’s browser, and browsers keep it separate per browser and per website. A chat started on willowpack.com won’t show on an Unbounce page (and vice versa), and it won’t follow the customer to their phone. Their quote email link always works: it reopens the chat on the page where it started.
    
        
    Can I put several boxes or buttons on one page?
          
    Yes. Paste the <div> wherever you want each one and the script once. They all share one chat, so a visitor never ends up with two conversations.
    
        
    Will Unbounce styles break it, or will it break my page?
          
    No. The box is drawn in an isolated layer (Shadow DOM), so page CSS can’t reach it and its styles can’t leak out. It adds nothing to the page except window.WillowpackChat.
    
        
    Can I open the chat from my own button?
          
    Yes, with a tiny bit of JavaScript after the script loads:
    
          
    <a href="#" onclick="WillowpackChat.open({ format: 'pouches' }); return false;">Quote pouches</a>
<a href="#" onclick="WillowpackChat.open({ q: '5,000 coffee bags' }); return false;">Price coffee bags</a>
<a href="#" onclick="WillowpackChat.openQuotes(); return false;">My quotes</a>
    
          
    Formats: pouches, labels, boxes, rollstock, help. Analytics can listen for willowpack:session and willowpack:quote events on window.
    
        
    Troubleshooting
          
      
            
    • Nothing shows where the box should be: check the script is added (Page Properties → Javascripts) and that you published after adding it. Unbounce’s editor doesn’t run scripts; use Preview.
      • 
            
    • The box shows but is cut off: make the Custom HTML element taller in Unbounce. The box needs about 220px of height (more on mobile when the format buttons wrap).
      • 
            
    • Campaign isn’t in Salesforce: the campaign is saved on the first message of a chat. Test in a private window so you start a fresh chat.
      • 
            
    • Resume link opens the page but not the chat: the script must be on the page the link points to.
      • 
            
    • Still stuck? Open the page with ?wp_debug=1 and check the browser console for [willowpack] lines.
